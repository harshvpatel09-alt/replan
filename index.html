A planner that does the arithmetic for you

You describe your term once. Replan works out what fits where, and keeps working it out every time something changes.

It moves work you missed

Skip Wednesday and the plan rebuilds overnight. The remaining work spreads across the days you have left, before the deadline rather than after it. Nothing is dropped and no due date quietly slides.

It builds the schedule

Give it your classes, what is due and roughly how long each thing takes. It fills your free hours with blocks sized to the work, earliest deadline first.

It knows the AP calendar

All 43 courses for 2026–27, with delivery mode, portfolio deadlines and every date in the May 2027 administration. Where College Board has not confirmed something, Replan says so rather than guessing.

It learns from what you finish

Tick blocks off as you go. Practice results feed back in, so the topics you keep getting wrong turn into review sessions on your schedule.

It reads your own material

Drop in a lecture PDF, a slide deck or a photo of your notes and get flashcards and questions built from that text — every word taken from your file, never invented.

It imports from your school

Google Classroom, Canvas, Schoology and twenty more. Paste a list, drop a screenshot, or hand it a calendar feed and your assignments arrive with their due dates attached.

Falling behind stops being a crisis

Every other planner hands you a schedule and blames you for breaking it. Replan treats a missed block as new information about your week.
